# E-Commerce Delivery Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

A Business Analyst project using the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset** to analyse delivery performance, identify where delays are concentrated, and understand their association with customer satisfaction.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The e-commerce operations team lacks clear visibility into where delivery delays are occurring and whether delayed orders are associated with lower customer satisfaction.

## ❓ Key Business Questions

1. What is the number of eligible delivered orders?
2. How many orders were delivered on time vs delayed?
3. How severe are delivery delays on average?
4. Which customer states have the highest volume of delays?
5. Are delayed orders associated with lower customer satisfaction?

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Excel – Data Profiling
- PostgreSQL & SQL – Data Analysis
- Power BI – Dashboard & Visualization
- Word – Business Documentation

## 📊 Key Findings

- **96,470** eligible delivered orders analysed
- **6.77%** of orders were delayed
- Average delay among delayed orders: **10.62 days**
- **SP and RJ** accounted for approximately **50.7%** of delayed orders
- Average review score:
  - Delayed: **2.27/5**
  - On Time: **4.29/5**

## 💡 Recommendations

- Prioritise **SP and RJ** for further operational investigation
- Evaluate regions using **delay volume, delay rate, and average delay**
- Monitor customer satisfaction alongside delivery performance
